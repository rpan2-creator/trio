PRD 01 · PIXEL PET GAME

PIP — your pixel pal

Hatch a mystery egg, design any creature you can imagine, or make a companion inspired by a pet who lives far away. Pet it, feed it, bathe it, teach it tricks, dress it up and play mini-games together — it never gets sick and never makes you feel guilty for leaving.

  • Endless species from a procedural pixel-creature generator
  • Rich direct play: rub, feed by drag, scrub, fetch, tricks, tickles
  • Three mini-games, daily quests, levels, shop and room decorating
  • Talks back, and only remembers what you confirm
